# ABS Trademark & Branding

## About ABS

**ABS** stands for **Azures Bot Service**.

The Apache License 2.0 does **not** grant permission to use project names, trademarks, service marks, logos, or branding. This file explains the branding rules for ABS.

## What you may do

You may:

- Use ABS in your own projects and services.
- Modify ABS for your own needs.
- Run your own hosted instance of ABS.
- Charge for your own hosting, support, or other services around your use of ABS.
- Refer to ABS as the software you are using, provided you do not imply that your service is the official ABS service.

## What you may not do

Without permission, you may not:

- Claim that you created the original ABS project.
- Present a modified or hosted version as the **official ABS** service.
- Use the **ABS** name, logo, or branding in a way that suggests your service is operated, endorsed, or officially affiliated with AzureMC-projects.
- Market your service as an official ABS service.
- Remove or replace the original copyright, license, or attribution notices required by the Apache License 2.0.
- Use the ABS name or branding to mislead users about the origin of your version.

You are welcome to say that your project **uses ABS** or is **based on ABS**, as long as the statement is accurate and does not imply official affiliation.

## Your own branding

If you create a hosted service or modified version, you should use your own service name, branding, and logo.

For example:

> "MyBot is powered by a modified version of ABS."

is acceptable.

Calling it:

> "Official ABS Hosting"

is not permitted without permission.

## Permission

If you want to use the ABS name, logo, or branding beyond the uses described above, contact the project owner for permission.
